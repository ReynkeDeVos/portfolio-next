# Defer a linked-entry module until a fourth assembly or a repeated fix

Whole-entry links are assembled by hand in three places: featured and supporting Projects in Selected work, and the Career Section's entries. Each combines a linked `Item` or `Card`, a `StretchedLink`, a `LinkArrow` and the new-tab notice from `section-parts.tsx`. A module that takes the destination and owns those parts was considered in the October 2026 architecture review. We deferred it: the three assemblies are consistent, the shared focus and hover treatment already lives in one place (`linkedItem`), and the history shows changes made once in the shared parts rather than the same fix repeated in each assembly. Revisit when a fourth kind of linked entry appears or a fix has to be made in all three.
