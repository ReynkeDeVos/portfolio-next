1. **Web:** TypeScript, React, modern CSS.
2. **Backend:** C#/.NET, Node.js, Python, APIs and data.

A deeper Skills view can expand these into **Languages**, **Web**, **APIs and data**, **Quality and delivery**, and **AI and automation**. Include framework/data/testing names from the tables only where they explain relevant work. Avoid proficiency percentages and an exhaustive package-logo wall.

Keep instructor responsibilities in career history: teaching provides evidence of breadth, but the primary identity remains software developer. Separate **used in projects**, **taught**, and **currently exploring** where that distinction matters. The coming-soon Better Auth work belongs in the final category. Effect and TanStack Start are chosen for the new portfolio; this inventory does not establish that they are taught.

The owner wants copy about trying new technologies, a terminal-first Linux workflow, daily AI news and choosing tools for actual usefulness. A small task/model/thinking-level recommendation list should come from the maintained model guide and be dated, rather than being inferred from old lecture model names.
