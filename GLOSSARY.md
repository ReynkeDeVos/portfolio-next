# Portfolio

Renke Brixel's bilingual developer portfolio: one page that introduces the owner and their work to recruiters and developers, in English and German.

## Language

**Locale**:
One of the languages the portfolio is published in, English or German, each with its own address.
_Avoid_: language (in code), lang

**Content**:
Facts about the owner that would stay true on any site: projects, career, skills, teaching, portrait, and how generic technology names translate.
_Avoid_: data, portfolio data

**Copy**:
The page's own words: labels, headings, notes, the not-found text and sentence templates that wrap Content.
_Avoid_: translations, strings, i18n

**Section**:
One of the four tabs the page is divided into: Work, Career, Skills and Workflow. Work is the default.
_Avoid_: tab, panel, page

**Project**:
An entry in the catalog of the owner's projects. A Project can exist without being shown.
_Avoid_: repo, work item

**Selected work**:
The Projects the owner chose to show, in their chosen order, split into featured and supporting.
_Avoid_: showcase, highlights

**Current role**:
The owner's present job, shown in the profile summary.
_Avoid_: current job, position

**Portrait viewer**:
The dialog that opens the larger portrait from the thumbnail.
_Avoid_: lightbox, photo modal

**Engine note**:
The dismissible notice shown outside Chromium-based browsers explaining that the portfolio targets Chromium.
_Avoid_: browser warning, compatibility banner
